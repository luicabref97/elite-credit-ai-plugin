Start a fresh credit journey. Whether your goal is to fix problems on your report, improve a clean score, build credit from scratch, or just understand and maintain what you have — this command figures out where you are and gives you a personalized plan.

## What you'll need (helpful but not all required)

- **Your credit report** in PDF — improves the analysis a lot. If you don't have one yet, you can download all 3 buros for free at [annualcreditreport.com](https://annualcreditreport.com). If you're not sure how to download it, just say so during the intake and I'll guide you step by step.
- **An idea of your goal** — buy a home, buy a car, build business credit, improve your score, or just maintain. You can pick more than one. If you're not sure, that's also fine.
- **A few minutes** to fill out a short intake form — language preference, your situation in your own words, score range, what's on your report (or "I'm not sure — analyze it for me"), and your state.

## What happens

The `flow-router` agent will:

1. **Ask you which language you prefer** (Español o English) — everything afterwards is in that language.
2. **Recover any prior journey state** if you've been here before — your progress is saved across sessions, so you don't have to re-explain.
3. **Show a short intake form** — multi-select goals, your situation in your own words, score range, what's on your report (or "no estoy seguro" if you don't know), and your state.
4. **Run a deep analysis** of your report if you uploaded one.
5. **Decide your route**: Repair / Optimization / Maintenance + Education, with a specific stage and realistic timing in weeks.
6. **Save your route** so future sessions resume exactly where you stopped.
7. **Communicate the plan**: friendly diagnosis, what we'll do this stage, expected score impact range, and ask if you want to start the first step now.

## After completion

You'll receive:

- A friendly diagnosis with your score, what's going on with your credit, and what we found that needs attention.
- Two outputs:
  - A **technical forensic report** (`output/forensic_report.md`) for credit-repair pros or for when you want depth.
  - A **consumer dashboard** (`output/consumer_dashboard.md`) — same analysis but in plain language, ready to read on its own.
- A short interactive Q&A at the end where I ask you about each problematic account — letters you've received, calls, what happened, documents you may have. This makes the dispute letters far stronger if you choose to proceed. You can skip any question.
- Your assigned flow + stage with a realistic timeline.
- An offer to start the first concrete action right away.

## Coming back later

Use `/next-step` to resume from any device — your journey state lives in Cowork Memoria server-side. The system will detect what's overdue or due today, what needs your attention, and what action to take next. If your situation changes (identity theft, score drop, completed goal), it auto-detects and recommends re-routing.

## Notes

- This command is the entry point for **new users**. If you're returning, `/next-step` is faster.
- Cowork's `Programado` (scheduled tasks) can automate check-ins so you don't have to remember to come back.
- This is educational guidance, **not legal advice**. For formal legal action, the system can refer you to NACA ([consumeradvocates.org](https://consumeradvocates.org)) — a free directory of consumer-law specialists.
