# Key Credit Law Statutes Reference

## FCRA — Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681)

### Reporting Limits
- **§ 605(a)(1)**: Bankruptcies — 10 years from filing date
- **§ 605(a)(2)**: Civil suits/judgments — 7 years from entry or SOL (whichever longer)
- **§ 605(a)(4)**: Paid tax liens — 7 years from payment
- **§ 605(a)(5)**: Collections/charge-offs — 7 years from DOFD
- **§ 605(c)**: DOFD = date of first delinquency + 180 days

### Accuracy & Disputes
- **§ 607(b)**: CRAs must follow reasonable procedures for maximum possible accuracy
- **§ 611(a)(1)**: 30-day reinvestigation requirement after consumer dispute
- **§ 611(a)(5)(A)**: If item cannot be verified within 30 days, CRA must delete it
- **§ 611(a)(6)**: CRA must notify furnisher of dispute within 5 business days
- **§ 611(a)(7)**: CRA must provide results of reinvestigation within 5 business days

### Furnisher Obligations
- **§ 623(a)(1)(A)**: Must not furnish info known to be inaccurate
- **§ 623(a)(2)**: Must correct/delete inaccurate info and notify CRAs
- **§ 623(a)(8)**: Direct-to-furnisher dispute right (since 2010)
- **§ 623(b)**: After CRA notification of dispute, furnisher must investigate

### Damages
- **§ 616**: Willful noncompliance — $100-$1,000 statutory damages + punitive + attorney fees
- **§ 617**: Negligent noncompliance — actual damages + attorney fees

## FDCPA — Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (15 U.S.C. § 1692)

### Validation & Communication
- **§ 809(a)**: Initial communication must include amount, creditor name, dispute rights
- **§ 809(b)**: 30-day validation right — collector must cease until validated
- **§ 805(c)**: Consumer can demand cessation of all communication
- **§ 805(b)**: Collector cannot contact third parties (except to locate debtor)

### Prohibited Practices
- **§ 807**: False/misleading representations
- **§ 808(1)**: Cannot collect more than amount owed
- **§ 806**: Harassment or abuse prohibited

### Damages
- **§ 1692k**: Up to $1,000 per violation + actual damages + attorney fees

## Key Case Law

### Henson v. CSC Credit Services (1st Cir. 1994)
- Established Notice-Activation Theory
- Once CRA receives dispute notice, heightened duty to investigate
- Cannot simply parrot furnisher's response

### Cushman v. Trans Union (3rd Cir. 1997)
- Two-Tier FCRA standard: (1) reasonable procedures, (2) maximum possible accuracy
- CRA must go beyond mechanical verification

### Johnson v. MBNA America Bank (4th Cir. 2004)
- Furnisher investigation under § 623(b) must be reasonable
- Cannot be cursory or perfunctory

### Cortez v. Trans Union (3rd Cir. 2010)
- Mixed/merged files — CRA liable for failing to separate consumer files
- Reasonable procedures requirement extends to file maintenance

### Safeco Ins. Co. v. Burr (S.Ct. 2007)
- Defined "willful" noncompliance as reckless disregard
- Objective standard for willfulness
