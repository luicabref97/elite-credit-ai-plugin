# Dispute Letter Frameworks

## Bureau Dispute Letter (FCRA 611)

```
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Date]

[Bureau Name]
[Bureau Address]

RE: Dispute of Inaccurate Information — Account #[XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-1234]

Dear Sir/Madam:

I am writing pursuant to the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1681i (Section 611),
to formally dispute inaccurate information appearing on my credit report.

ACCOUNT IN DISPUTE:
Creditor: [Name]
Account Number: [masked]
Reported Status: [current status]
Bureau: [bureau name]

SPECIFIC INACCURACIES:
[Detail each specific error with data points]

LEGAL BASIS:
[Cite specific FCRA/FDCPA sections]

REQUESTED ACTION:
I request that you investigate this matter within 30 days as required by FCRA Section
611(a)(1), and [DELETE/CORRECT] the above-referenced information.

Please be advised that under FCRA Section 611(a)(5)(A), if you cannot verify the
accuracy of this information within 30 days, you must delete it from my credit file.

I reserve all rights under FCRA Sections 616 and 617 regarding willful and negligent
noncompliance.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
[Name]
SSN Last 4: [XXXX]
DOB: [Date]

Enclosures: [List supporting documents]
```

## Debt Validation Letter (FDCPA 809(b))

```
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[Date]

[Collection Agency Name]
[Agency Address]

RE: Debt Validation Request — Account #[XXXX]

Dear Sir/Madam:

Pursuant to the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1692g(b) (Section 809(b)),
I hereby request validation of the above-referenced debt.

Please provide:
1. The original signed credit agreement bearing my signature
2. Complete chain of title from original creditor to your agency
3. Itemized accounting of the balance claimed, including:
   - Original principal balance
   - Each fee, interest charge, or penalty added
   - All payments received and applied
4. Proof that the statute of limitations has not expired
5. Proof of your license to collect in [State]

Until this debt is validated, you must cease all collection activity pursuant to
FDCPA Section 809(b) and must not report this debt to any credit reporting agency.

Any continued collection activity or credit reporting prior to validation constitutes
a violation of the FDCPA, for which I may seek statutory damages of up to $1,000 per
violation under 15 U.S.C. § 1692k.

Sincerely,
[Name]
```

## Goodwill Removal Letter

```
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[Date]

[Creditor Name]
Customer Relations Department
[Address]

RE: Goodwill Removal Request — Account #[XXXX]

Dear Customer Relations:

I am writing to request a goodwill adjustment to my credit reporting for the
above-referenced account. I have been a valued customer of [Creditor] for [X years]
and take my financial obligations seriously.

[Explain circumstances that led to the negative item — be brief and factual]

Since that time, I have:
- [Brought account current / paid in full]
- [Maintained on-time payments for X months]
- [Any other positive actions]

I respectfully request that you consider removing the [late payment / collection]
from my credit report as a goodwill gesture. This adjustment would significantly
help me [goal: purchase a home, refinance, etc.].

I understand this is a discretionary decision and I appreciate your consideration
of my request.

Thank you for your time and continued service.

Sincerely,
[Name]
Account Number: [XXXX]
```
